Privacy & scope

VITALS+ is a wellness and fitness tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose conditions or prescribe treatment. BMI and the estimates shown (BMR, body fat %, hydration targets) are widely used general heuristics, not clinical measurements. If you have health concerns, please talk to a qualified healthcare provider.
